## What's owed to close the near tier

Discharge `Owed.degree.ramificationSumEqualsDegree_statement`:

```
∀ y : Y, ∑ x ∈ (fibre y).toFinset, manifoldRamificationIndex f x = degreeFiber f hf
```

for non-constant `ContMDiff f`. This is the Riemann-Hurwitz total-weight
identity. The proof needs four ingredients composed together:

1. Disjoint open neighbourhoods around fibre points (`Set.Finite.t2_separation`
   in mathlib + ZZ48 `fibres_finite_statement_holds_unconditional` for finiteness).
2. Manifold-side k-fold via chart pullback at each fibre point: lift
   `Manifold/LocalKFoldMultiplicityFullyUnconditional.lean`'s planar statement
   through `chartAt ℂ` and identify the resulting `k` with
   `manifoldRamificationIndex f x`.
3. Compactness argument: for `w` in a small enough punctured nbhd of `y`,
   *every* preimage of `w` lies in the union of disjoint nbhds (otherwise
   take a sequence `w_n → y` of preimages outside the cover; by compactness
   it has a limit point, which by continuity sits in the fibre, contradicting
   "outside the cover").
4. Cardinality identification via the corrected `fibre_card_well_defined_at_regular_statement`
   (ZZ176): for a regular `w` near `y`, count of preimages = `degreeFiber f hf`.

Each ingredient builds on infrastructure that's already in the repo (charts,
analyticity bridges, ZZ48, ZZ176). The analysis-flavored pieces (especially
the compactness argument and the manifold-side k-fold lift) are non-trivial.
